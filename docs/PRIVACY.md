# Privacy and Operator Information

## Product privacy model

Employer accounts are authenticated. Candidates can apply without creating Brandvika accounts. Candidate names, contact details, answers and CVs are private application information and must be visible only to the relevant employer and authorized platform operations.

## Data categories expected in the MVP

- Employer account identity and authentication metadata.
- Employer profile, brand assets and verification information.
- Job descriptions and application form configuration.
- Candidate-submitted contact information, application answers and CV files.
- Consent records, application status history and employer notes.
- Security, abuse-reporting and audit metadata.

## Required controls before launch

- Explain each collection purpose at the point of collection.
- Record the applicable privacy notice version and consent timestamp.
- Use private storage for CVs and verification files.
- Provide a documented process for access, correction, deletion and complaints.
- Define retention periods and verified deletion jobs.
- Minimize access to applicant information and keep audit records for sensitive actions.
- Publish the actual operator identity, contact channel and grievance process.

## Legal status

This document is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. It does not claim compliance with India's DPDP Act or any other law. The platform owner must supply accurate operator details and obtain qualified legal review before public launch.
